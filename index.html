How to Use LotCalc Pro

1. Account Type
Choose your account type — Standard, Mini, Micro, Nano, or Cent. This scales your pip value correctly.

2. Balance & Pair
Enter your account balance in USD. Select your trading pair — pip values auto-update.

3. Risk %
Recommended: 1-2% max per trade. Use quick pills or drag the slider.

4. Stop Loss
Enter your SL in pips. For Gold (XAUUSD): 1 pip = $0.10 price movement.

Formula:
Lot = Risk$ ÷ (SL pips × Pip Value)
